# Diet cheat-sheet

## Vegetarian
- **Out:** meat, poultry, fish, seafood, gelatin, lard, animal stocks, fish or oyster sauce, dashi.
- **Hidden:** Worcestershire sauce (contains anchovy); Parmesan, Pecorino, Grana Padano and Gorgonzola (made with animal rennet); some candies (gelatin).
- **Swaps:** vegetarian Worcestershire, vegetarian hard cheese, agar-agar, kombu + shiitake stock (not for low-FODMAP or Jain).

## Vegan (includes vegetarian)
- **Out:** dairy (milk, butter, ghee, cream, cheese, paneer, yogurt, whey, casein), eggs, mayo, honey.
- **Swaps:**

| instead of | use |
|---|---|
| paneer | pressed firm tofu |
| ghee or butter | oil or vegan butter |
| cream | coconut cream or cashew cream |
| yogurt | coconut or soy yogurt |
| egg (binding) | flax egg: 1 Tbsp ground flax + 3 Tbsp water |
| egg (foam) | aquafaba |
| honey | maple syrup or date syrup |

- **Nutrients to cover:** B12 (supplement or fortified foods), iron with vitamin C, omega-3 (flax, chia, walnuts), calcium, iodine, protein at every meal.

## Low-FODMAP (Monash University; check the app for current serves)
- **Out (high):**
  - garlic and onion in every form, including powders; leek bulbs; the white parts of spring onions
  - apples, pears, mango, watermelon, dried fruit, honey, agave
  - cauliflower, button mushrooms, asparagus, artichoke
  - kidney beans, black beans, baked beans, silken tofu, soy milk made from whole beans
  - cashews, pistachios
  - wheat, rye or barley in normal serves; regular milk, yogurt, ricotta
- **Fine:**
  - garlic-infused oil; green tops of spring onion and leek; chives; asafoetida
  - firm tofu, tempeh
  - rice, quinoa, oats (up to ½ cup), polenta, spelt sourdough
  - carrot, zucchini, eggplant, bell pepper, green beans, bok choy, spinach, potato
  - oranges, kiwi, pineapple, strawberries
  - maple syrup
  - lactose-free dairy and hard cheeses
  - peanuts, walnuts, macadamias, pumpkin seeds
- **Portion-limited:**
  - canned and rinsed chickpeas: about ¼ cup per person
  - canned lentils: about ½ cup per person
  - avocado: about 30 g
  - sweet potato: about ½ cup
  - almonds: about 10
  - butternut squash: small serves
  - canned coconut milk: about ½ cup
  - beetroot: about 2 slices
  - celery: a small piece
- **Note:** the elimination phase is temporary and best done with a dietitian.

## Gluten-free
- **Out:** wheat (including maida, atta, semolina/sooji/rava, durum, spelt, farro, bulgur, couscous, seitan), barley, rye, malt, malt vinegar, brewer's yeast, beer; regular bread, pasta, noodles, tortillas, naan or roti; regular soy, teriyaki and hoisin sauces.
- **Check the label:** oats (must be certified GF), asafoetida (usually cut with wheat), stock cubes, miso, curry pastes, soba (often part wheat).
- **Fine:** rice, quinoa, millet (bajra), sorghum (jowar), ragi, buckwheat, amaranth, corn and polenta, besan, tamari, coconut aminos, rice noodles, rice paper.

## Nut-free
- **Out:** all tree nuts and peanuts, including their milks, butters, flours and oils; marzipan, praline, Nutella, frangipane; pesto (usually contains pine nuts); satay.
- **Usually fine (confirm for the person):** coconut, nutmeg, water chestnut, butternut squash.
- **Swaps:** sunflower or pumpkin seeds, sunflower seed butter, tahini (if no sesame allergy), melon seeds (magaz) for Indian gravies, oat or soy milk.

## Jain (includes vegetarian)
- **Out:**
  - onion, garlic, leek, spring onion, chives
  - root and underground vegetables: potato, sweet potato, carrot, beetroot, radish, turnip, yam, taro, cassava, fresh ginger, fresh turmeric
  - mushrooms and other fungi, yeast
  - eggs, honey, alcohol
- **Allowed in most families:** dairy (paneer, ghee, yogurt), dry ginger powder (sonth), turmeric powder, asafoetida, raw banana, gourds, pumpkin, cabbage, capsicum, tomatoes, legumes.
- **Practice notes:** many Jains avoid eating after sunset, and some avoid green leafy vegetables on certain days (tithi). Ask before assuming.

## Sattvic (includes vegetarian)
- **Out:** onion, garlic, leek, mushrooms, eggs, alcohol, caffeine, vinegar and heavily fermented condiments; very hot chili.
- **Favoured:** fresh fruit and vegetables (root vegetables are allowed), whole grains, mung dal, ghee, milk, nuts and seeds, honey, jaggery, mild spices (cumin, coriander, turmeric, fennel, cardamom), ginger.

## Combination quick-table

| combo | biggest challenge | go-to building blocks |
|---|---|---|
| vegan + low-FODMAP | protein without beans | firm tofu, tempeh, small portions of canned lentils, quinoa, peanuts |
| vegan + gluten-free | baking, binding | buckwheat, besan, flax egg, psyllium |
| low-FODMAP + gluten-free | mostly aligned | rice, quinoa, oats (GF), polenta |
| Jain + vegan | dairy-heavy recipes | tofu "paneer", coconut yogurt, oil |
| Jain + low-FODMAP | portions of legumes | asafoetida (GF), capsicum, gourds, tomatoes, firm tofu, hard cheeses |
| nut-free + vegan | creamy sauces | coconut milk, seed creams, oat cream |
